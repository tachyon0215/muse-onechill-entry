# muse-onechill-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line, One Chill](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-one-line-one-chill-6d4d5488).

## The entry

> At 3 a.m. the baby monitor played my own voice singing her lullaby from inside her dark room, note-perfect - while I stood in the hallway, holding her, humming nothing.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: Dread that outlasts the line: the voice is hers, the room is empty-but-for-the-sound, and the final clause (holding her, humming nothing) removes every safe explanation without gore or a stock mirror/doll/basement image.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 251627a98a26bb7249c5a7d61fbbf8807b6eff94ec109b7cbc8e2378b8c8c802).
